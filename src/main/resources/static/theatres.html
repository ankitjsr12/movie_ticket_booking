Theatres in Kolkata

Rich History of Kolkata's Cinema Culture

Star Theatre

Est. 1883

Kolkata's oldest surviving commercial theatre. Once the heart of Bengali theatre and film. Many legendary plays and early films were screened here.

Metro Cinema

Est. 1934

Iconic Art Deco style theatre. One of the most beautiful cinemas in India. Still loved for its grand architecture and classic film screenings.

Nandan

Est. 1985

Government-run cultural complex named after Rabindranath Tagore. Major centre for film festivals, art cinema and cultural events in Kolkata.

PVR INOX South City Mall

Modern Multiplex

Most popular modern multiplex in South Kolkata. Known for comfortable seating, latest Hollywood & Bollywood releases.

Cinepolis Acropolis

Modern Multiplex

Premium multiplex with luxury recliners and excellent sound system. Very popular among young audience.

RDB Cinemas

Modern Multiplex

Located in Salt Lake, known for its big screens and family-friendly environment.
